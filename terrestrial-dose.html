European Data Sources

  • GLiM Global Lithology Map — ~1.5 km global lithology polygons
  • BRGM French Geological Survey — 1:1M–1:50K geology WMS for France
  • BGS British Geological Survey — 1:50K bedrock geology for UK & Ireland
  • GEM Global Active Faults — fault proximity and lineament density
  • SoilGrids 2.0 — 250 m soil permeability and texture
  • Copernicus GLO-30 DEM — 30 m elevation for lineament extraction
  • EURDEP — European real-time gamma dose rate monitoring network
