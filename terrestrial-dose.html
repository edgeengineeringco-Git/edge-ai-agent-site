Terrestrial Dose Indicator — Standards & Provenance

Architecture

Geology is the primary predictor. OneGeology lithology polygons are mapped to typical K/U/Th activities via a 35-class lookup table, then through UNSCEAR/ICRP dose models. Measurements (airborne radiometric, geochemical) override the prior where available.

OneGeology polygon → lithology class → LITHOLOGY_ACTIVITIES prior (K/U/Th Bq/kg)
→ [if radiometric survey] OVERRIDE prior
→ external gamma + GRP (radon) + GTP (thoron) → dose → risk tier
→ [if measured CRn/CTn] override geogenic estimates

Dose Models

  • External gamma: Ḋair = 0.462·ARa + 0.604·ATh + 0.041·AK (nGy/h); Eext = Ḋair × 8760 × 0.8 × 0.7 × 10⁻⁶
  • Geogenic Radon Potential (GRP): from Ra-226 + permeability + fault proximity + lineament density + lithology factor
  • Global Thoron Potential (GTP): from Th-232 → emanation (0.1–0.3) → indoor gas → EEC (×0.02) → dose (×40 nSv × 7000 h). Calibrated: world-average soil → 0.10 mSv/yr
  • Radon dose: EU BSS method (default): 300 Bq/m³ → 10 mSv/yr; ICRP 137; UNSCEAR; ICRP 65
