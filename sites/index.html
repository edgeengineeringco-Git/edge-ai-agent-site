2 · What you are looking at

Every image is the genuine retrieved data (Planetary Computer STAC / NASA GIBS), georeferenced and overlaid on its true geographic bounds. The 2002, 2005 and 2008 Landsat crops are pixel-co-registered (identical bounding box), so toggling between them is a like-for-like comparison. The red squares mark the 16×16 px blocks with the largest local luminance change 2002→2008 (see table). They cluster along the north-west shoreline / estuary margin — consistent with tidal / vegetation change, not a discrete object.

4 · Limitations

  • Resolution. Landsat 30 m / MODIS 250 m cannot resolve a drum. Change layers show large-area change only.
  • Drum-scale = commercial. Only ~0.25–1 m 2002–2008 coverage is order-only air photography: OSNI / Land & Property Services (start here), Bluesky, Getmapping, NCAP. Not freely downloadable.
  • No visual verification. Change layers are computed from decoded pixels; confirm visually with a human.
  • No determination. Nothing here infers burial, concealment, or intent.
