Who We Are

A Research Team at the Forefront of Geoengineering

Earthwise Dynamics Geo Environs (EDGE) is an Irish SME delivering advanced solutions in geotechnical engineering, environmental assessment, and geospatial analysis.

Based in Maynooth, we collaborate with Trinity College Dublin, UCD, and DIAS, and have access to environmental laboratories across Europe. As an ESA incubatee, we develop smart critical mineral detection tools for terrestrial and space applications.

Our approach is distinct: we integrate AI, big data processing, and advanced geospatial methods to deliver precise, impactful results.

Funded Research Projects

Gamma Radiation & Radon/Thoron Emission in Irish Building Materials

GIS / Epidemiology

GIS & Spatiotemporal Analysis of SARS-CoV-2 Using Multiple Datasets

Geospatial model predicting contagion and lethality rates using 40+ parameters including atmospheric pollutants, meteorological variables, and lockdown phases.
