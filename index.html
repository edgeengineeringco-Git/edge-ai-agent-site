Who We Are

A Research Team at the Forefront of Geoengineering

Earthwise Dynamics Geo Environs (EDGE) is an Irish SME delivering advanced solutions in geotechnical engineering, environmental assessment, and geospatial analysis. Based at Maynooth University, we collaborate with leading institutions across Ireland and Europe.

As an ESA incubatee, we develop smart critical mineral detection tools for terrestrial and space applications, alongside technologies for mapping, monitoring, and geoscience software.

European Space Agency incubatee — bridging Earth and space
Access to environmental laboratories across Europe
State-of-the-art sensors, drones, and analytical tools
Funded by IRC, GSI, EOSC, and Enterprise Ireland
Recent Work

Funded Research Projects

Our work is recognised and funded by leading research institutions across Ireland and Europe.

Radiometric

Gamma Radiation & Radon/Thoron Emission in Irish Building Materials

Quantifying radiation exposure from naturally occurring radioactive materials in construction across Ireland — residential and commercial buildings.

🏛️ IRC & Geological Survey Ireland · Sept 2024
GIS / Epidemiology

GIS & Spatiotemporal Analysis of SARS-CoV-2 Using Multiple Datasets

Geospatial model predicting contagion and lethality rates using 40+ parameters — atmospheric pollutants, meteorological variables, sociodemographic factors, and lockdown phases.

🏛️ European Open Science Cloud · May 2024
Multi-Hazard Platform

Web-Based Geodatabase for Natural Hazard Assessment & Multi-Hazard Mapping

Platform integrating 200+ geospatial variables with 60M+ data points for modelling landslide, flood, erosion, radon, and climate susceptibility at 500m resolution.

🏛️ Enterprise Ireland · Jan 2024
