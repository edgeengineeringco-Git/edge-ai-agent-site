What We Do
Capabilities built for
Earth and space
From terrestrial mineral exploration to lunar prospecting — AI-powered geointelligence.

Critical Mineral Prospecting

Terrestrial and extraterrestrial REE exploration. Smart detection tools for lunar and Martian missions. AI-powered gamma-ray and geochemical analysis.

Advanced Drone Surveys

UAV-mounted gamma-ray spectrometry, pXRF, hyperspectral imaging, thermal cameras, and LiDAR with customised sensor payloads for high-resolution site characterisation.

AI Automation & Software

ML models automating geotechnical reports, anomaly detection, and resource estimation. Custom geoscience software and the AstroTerra Intelligence Platform.

Earth Observation

Satellite remote sensing for environmental monitoring, land-use classification, and resource mapping. ESA Copernicus and Sentinel integration.

Geoengineering

Geospatial modelling, multi-hazard susceptibility mapping, slope stability, and foundation assessment. 200+ variables at 500m resolution.

Climate & Carbon Intelligence

Carbon reduction strategies, climate impact monitoring, and NORM radiation assessment for building materials and environmental compliance.
