What We Do

Our Capabilities

From critical mineral exploration to climate resilience — we deploy cutting-edge technology to solve complex geoengineering and environmental challenges.

Renewable Energy & Critical Mineral Resourcing

Exploration and assessment of rare earth elements and critical minerals using advanced geophysical and geochemical survey techniques.

Earth Observation Data Analysis

Satellite remote sensing and Earth observation analytics for environmental monitoring, land-use classification, and resource mapping.

Specialised UAV Surveys

Drone-mounted gamma-ray spectrometry, pXRF, and hyperspectral imaging for high-resolution site characterisation and mineral targeting.

Geospatial Modelling & Mapping

GIS-based spatial analysis, multi-hazard susceptibility mapping, and geodatabase platforms for informed decision-making.

Climate Change & Carbon Reduction

Monitoring climate change impacts and developing data-driven carbon reduction strategies for resilient, sustainable communities.

Software Development & Automation

Custom geoscience software, AI-driven automation tools, and web platforms for data processing and geointelligence.

Who We Are

A Research-Focused Team at the Forefront of Geoengineering

Earthwise Dynamics Geo Environs (EDGE) is an Irish SME delivering advanced solutions in geotechnical engineering, environmental assessment, and geospatial analysis. We collaborate with leading institutions including Trinity College Dublin, UCD, and DIAS.

As a European Space Agency incubatee, we develop smart critical mineral detection tools for terrestrial and space applications, as well as technologies for mapping, monitoring, carbon reduction, and geoscience software.

Recent Work

Funded Research Projects

Our work is recognised and funded by leading research institutions across Ireland and Europe.

Radiometric

Quantifying Gamma Radiation Exposure and Radon/Thoron Emission in Irish Building Materials

Comprehensive assessment of naturally occurring radioactive materials (NORM) in construction materials across Ireland, quantifying exposure pathways for residential and commercial buildings.

💰 Funded by IRC & Geological Survey Ireland · Sept 2024
GIS / Epidemiology

GIS & Spatiotemporal Analysis of SARS-CoV-2 — Using Multiple Datasets

Geospatial model predicting weekly contagion and lethality rates of COVID-19 in Ireland. Over 40 parameters including atmospheric pollutants, meteorological variables, sociodemographic factors, and lockdown phases used as model inputs.

💰 Funded by European Open Science Cloud (EOSC) · May 2024
Multi-Hazard Platform

Web-Based Geodatabase for Natural Hazard Assessment & Multi-Hazard Susceptibility Mapping

Advanced platform integrating 200+ geospatial variables with 60M+ data points for modelling landslide, flood, erosion, radon, and climate change susceptibility across Ireland at 500m resolution.

💰 Funded by Enterprise Ireland · Jan 2024
