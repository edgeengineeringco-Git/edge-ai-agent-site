About EDGE
A research-focused team
at the forefront of
geoengineering
Earthwise Dynamics Geo Environs (EDGE) is an Irish SME delivering advanced solutions in geotechnical engineering, environmental assessment, and geospatial analysis.

Bridging Earth and space through technology

Based at Maynooth University, we collaborate with leading institutions including Trinity College Dublin, UCD, and DIAS. As an ESA incubatee, we develop smart critical mineral detection tools for terrestrial and space applications.

Our team integrates AI, big data processing, and advanced geospatial methods to deliver precise, impactful results for clients across Europe.

What We Do
Capabilities built for
complex challenges
From critical mineral exploration to climate resilience — we deploy cutting-edge technology to solve the hardest problems in geoengineering and environmental science.

Renewable Energy & Critical Minerals

Site selection, feasibility studies, resource assessment, and environmental impact analysis for wind, geothermal, solar, and hydropower projects. Advanced exploration of rare earth elements.

Earth Observation

Satellite remote sensing and analytics for environmental monitoring, land-use classification, and resource mapping across Europe.

Specialised UAV Surveys

Drone-mounted gamma-ray spectrometry, pXRF, and hyperspectral imaging for high-resolution site characterisation and mineral targeting.

Geospatial Modelling & Mapping

GIS-based spatial analysis, multi-hazard susceptibility mapping, and geodatabase platforms for informed decision-making.

Climate Change & Carbon Reduction

Monitoring climate impacts and developing data-driven carbon reduction strategies for resilient, sustainable communities.

Software & Automation

Custom geoscience software, AI-driven automation tools, and web platforms for geospatial data analysis and visualization.
